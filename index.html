ABOUT

Throw Your Hands in the Air turns your webcam into a controller. It uses hand tracking, a computer vision technique that finds 21 points on each of your hands, built on Google's open-source MediaPipe. It runs right inside your browser and follows your fingers in real time, so you can wipe, pinch, grab and wave your way through a few playful experiments. It's safe to use. Nothing here is recorded or uploaded, there's no backend, and your video never leaves your computer. I made this to play with what hands can do on a screen, and built it with the help of AI. Pick any game below to begin, and if it's your first time, start with the warm up.
